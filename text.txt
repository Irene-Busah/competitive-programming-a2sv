Idea 3: Kazi-Match (AI Gig-Worker Profiler)

The Problem: Plumbers, electricians, and informal gig workers rely mostly on word of mouth. They often struggle to write professional CVs or online profiles to market their services to a broader audience.
The Solution: A platform that automatically builds professional mini-portfolios for gig workers through a simple chat.
The AI Component (NLP): A worker chats with the AI (e.g., "I know how to fix pipes and water tanks, I've done it for 3 years, I charge 10k an hour"). The LLM restructures this raw input into a highly professional, formatted profile, highlighting skills, generating an estimated rate card, and writing a catchy biography.
Chakra UI Build: A grid of beautiful profile cards (using Chakra's Avatar, Badge for skills, and Box structures). Users can click on a card to see the AI-generated professional profile and click a button to contact them via WhatsApp.



This is a fantastic pivot. Turning it into a two-sided marketplace (a connection platform) makes it a much stronger, more complete product for the judges. It shifts the project from a simple "resume builder tool" into a functional gig-economy platform.

Let’s refine the idea to include the booking flow while still keeping it realistic for a 12-hour build.

Here is how Kazi-Match (The Two-Sided AI Gig Marketplace) could work:

The Two-Sided Platform Flow
We will have two views in our React app: Worker Mode and Client Mode.

1. Worker Onboarding (The AI Profile Builder)

Action: A worker (e.g., an electrician) logs in and types a rough sentence: "I fix wires and install lights, 4 years doing this, I charge 15k a day."
AI Engine: The LLM instantly structures this into a verified profile, automatically tagging them with Electrician, generating a professional bio, and standardizing their rate.
2. Client Sourcing & AI Diagnostics (The "Smart Search")

Action: A homeowner in Kigali doesn't need to search through menus. They just use the AI search bar and type their problem: "My kitchen sink is leaking heavily and the pipe is split."
AI Engine: The LLM categorizes the problem (Skill Needed: Plumbing, Urgency: High), estimates how many hours it might take, and then immediately displays Chakra UI Card components showing the best local plumbers.
3. Frictionless Booking (The Hackathon Shortcut)

Action: The client clicks "Book [Worker Name]".
The Hackathon Trick: Building a full real-time chat and payment backend in 12 hours is extremely risky. Instead, the AI generates a structured "Work Order Summary" and we use a WhatsApp API redirect.
Result: When the client hits "Send Request", it opens WhatsApp between the client and the worker, pre-filling a highly professional message generated by the AI: "Hello Bosco, Kazi-Match matched us. I have an urgent plumbing issue (Kitchen Sink Pipe Split) in Kacyiru. Estimated job: 2 hours. Are you available today?"
Why this wins the Hackathon:
Real-World Usability: Integrating WhatsApp for the final connection proves you understand how business is actually done in Kigali right now.
Use of AI: You are using AI for two distinct features: (1) Standardizing informal worker data, and (2) Diagnosing client problems to match them with the right skills.
Product Quality: Using Chakra UI, we can build a stunning, mobile-first interface that looks like a funded startup.
Are you ready to start building this? If yes, I will create an official Implementation Plan encompassing the architecture, folder structure, and the exact steps to build the frontend and the AI integrations so we can hit the ground running!

Just one concern, how will we ensure the client or customer will always come back... considering after matching them, they could do business out of our platform? 

Also, how will we make money from this?
These are the exact questions the judges will ask you during the pitch. Addressing "Platform Leakage" (people taking the business offline) and "Monetization" is what separates a cool technical project from a winning business idea.

Here is how you answer both questions to the judges, and how we reflect it in our prototype:

1. How do we keep them on the platform? (Solving Leakage)
If they just exchange numbers, they might do business off-platform to avoid fees. You solve this by adding value that they cannot get if they leave the platform.

The "Kazi-Secure" Promise (Trust & Escrow): During the pitch, explain that the long-term vision is an integration with MTN Mobile Money (MoMo). Clients pay the platform upfront, and the money is held in escrow until the client clicks "Job Completed successfully." They stay on the platform for Safety & Dispute Resolution. If they go off-platform, they lose the guarantee.
The Reputation Engine for Workers: If a worker goes off-platform, they don't get a verified 5-Star Review on Kazi-Match. Plumbers want to be booked through the app so their profile ranks #1, allowing them to charge premium prices.
Hackathon Feature to Build: We build a simple "Review & Invoice" button on the dashboard. It shows that the platform handles the professional paperwork and rating system, proving why users stick around.
2. How do we make money? (The Business Model)
You have two very strong models you can pitch:

Option A: The Lead Generation Model (Pay-Per-Lead)
How it works: Joining is free for workers. But when the AI finds a client who urgently needs a plumber, the worker must pay a micro-fee (e.g., 500 RWF) to "unlock" the client's WhatsApp number and accept the job.
Why judges love it: It has zero barrier to entry for workers to sign up, but monetizes immediately when there is demand.
Option B: The SaaS Subscription (Kazi Pro)
How it works: Basic AI profiles are free. However, workers can pay 2,000 RWF a month for "Kazi Pro". Pro workers get bumped to the top of the AI search results, and they get access to an AI Invoice Generator to look more professional to rich clients.
Why judges love it: Predictable, recurring revenue.
